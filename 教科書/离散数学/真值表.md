下面的符号以数理逻辑为主。
# $\land$
同 $\cap$ ，`AND`

|     | T   | F   |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T   | T   | F   |
| F   | F   | F   |

---
# $\lor$
同 $\cup$ ，`OR`

|     | T   | F   |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T   | T   | T   |
| F   | T   | F   |

---
# $\lnot$
同 $\sim$ ，`NOT`

|     | T   | F   |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~   | F   | T   |

---
# $\implies$

| Arg1\0 | T   | F   |
| ------ | --- | --- |
| T      | T   | T   |
| F      | F   | T   |

---
# $\iff$

|     | T   | F   |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T   | T   | F   |
| F   | F   | T   |

---
# $\oplus$  
同`XOR`

|     | T   | F   |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T   | F   | T   |
| F   | T   | F   |
